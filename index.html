About Me

Designing experiences that matter

12+ years in product design, and I've stayed deeply hands-on the whole way — from the first piece of evidence through the shipped design. Discovery research, synthesis, information architecture, wireframes, interaction and visual design, prototypes, usability validation, developer handoff, and post-launch analysis. I own all of it.

My depth is in fintech, banking, and insurance — regulated environments where a design decision carries real financial and compliance consequence. JPMorganChase, Fidelity, and Prudential on the financial side; CVS Health and Lifely on insurance and benefits; plus enterprise SaaS, healthcare, and e-commerce work at Salesforce, Autodesk, CrowdStrike, and Walmart.

As an HFI Certified Usability Analyst (CUA) — fewer than 10,000 active holders globally — I plan and run mixed-methods research, then carry those findings all the way into pixels. Products I've designed serve 1,500+ business users and millions of consumers. The work is translating ambiguous customer, business, regulatory, and technical requirements into clear product direction and polished responsive web and mobile experiences.

I use Claude AI, Figma AI, and Microsoft Copilot to accelerate synthesis, concept exploration, workflow documentation, and prototype iteration — while applying human-centered judgment, accessibility standards, and design-system consistency to everything that ships. I influence roadmaps and executive decisions, and mentor designers around research-led craft. Open to Principal, Staff, and Senior UX / Product Designer roles — Full-Time or Contract — NYC Metro and Remote.
